The Citizen Creed

An identity you can adopt — no thinking required

Who am I?

You do not have to invent yourself from scratch. Take this creed. It is yours now.

This text is adapted from the official Estonian soldier's oath (kaitseväelase tõotus) — reshaped for ordinary life, for neighbors, parents, workers, students, and anyone asking who am I?

The Citizen Creed

I, a human being, swear to remain faithful to truth, dignity, and the people who depend on me; to defend what is good in myself and in others with all my reason and care; to be ready to sacrifice comfort and ego for what is right; to hold myself to inner discipline; and to honor my commitments honestly and steadily, knowing that if I do not, I alone bear the cost.

Source: Adapted from the Estonian Defence Forces military oath (Kaitseväeteenistuse seadus § 10), which reads in part: "I swear to remain faithful to the democratic Republic of Estonia… to defend with all my reason and strength… to observe discipline… and to perform all my duties precisely." Military language has been translated into the language of everyday citizenship.

Who Am I? — Say It Out Loud

You do not need a perfect answer. You need a standing answer. Use these:

  • I am someone who keeps my word.
  • I am someone who protects what is good — starting with myself.
  • I am someone who tells the truth, even when it costs me.
  • I am someone who shows up for the people who count on me.
  • I am someone who chooses discipline over impulse.
  • I am someone who pays the price of my own choices.
  • I am a citizen — not of a border, but of decency.

The Six Values

Taken from the Estonian Defence Forces Code of Ethics (Kaitseväe eetikakoodeks) — translated for daily life:

  • Honesty — Ausus I speak truth to myself and others. I do not hide behind excuses.
  • Courage — Vaprus I face what is hard. I do not run from necessary conversations or actions.
  • Competence — Asjatundlikkus I do my work well. I keep learning. I take my responsibilities seriously.
  • Loyalty — Ustavus I stay true to what I believe and to the people who trust me.
  • Cooperation — Koostöövalmidus I help when I can. I do not leave others to carry what we share.
  • Openness — Avatus I listen. I admit when I am wrong. I remain willing to grow.

Daily Practice — Three Minutes

Each morning, read the creed once. Then ask yourself one question:

What is one thing today that a person like me would do?

That is enough. Identity is not a mood. It is a pattern of choices.

What This Looks Like in Real Life

  • At home You are tired but your child needs attention. You show up anyway. That is the creed.
  • At work You could cut a corner. You do the honest thing. That is inner discipline.
  • With a friend They need hard truth, not comfort. You speak with care. That is courage.
  • Alone No one is watching. You still keep your promise to yourself. That is who you are.
  • When you fail You do not pretend. You own it, repair what you can, and begin again. That is paying the price.

Kodaniku Tõotus — Estonian Version

Kodaniku tõotus

Mina, inimene, tõotan jääda ustavaks tõele, väärikusele ja inimestele, kes minust sõltuvad; kaitsta head endas ja teistes kogu oma mõistuse ja hoole eest; olla valmis ohverdama mugavust ja ego selle eest, mis on õige; hoida kinni sisemisest distsipliinist; ning täita oma kohustusi ausalt ja järjepidevalt, teades, et vastasel korral maksan hinda ise.

Põhineb Eesti kaitseväelase ametlikul tõotusel (KVTS § 10), kohandatud igapäevaseks eluks.

Kes ma olen?

  • Olen inimene, kes peab oma sõna.
  • Olen inimene, kes kaitseb head — alustades iseendast.
  • Olen inimene, kes räägib tõtt, isegi kui see maksab.
  • Olen inimene, kes ilmub kohale nende jaoks, kes minust loodavad.
  • Olen inimene, kes valib distsipliini impulsi asemel.
  • Olen inimene, kes kannab oma valikute hinna.
  • Olen kodanik — mitte piiri, vaid väärikuse mõttes.
