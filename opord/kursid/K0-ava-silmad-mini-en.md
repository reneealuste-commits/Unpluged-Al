## Module 3 — Behavior profiling — see the person (~45 min)

**Source:** Chase Hughes — behavior analysis, nonverbal communication

**Watch (EN) — free entry points:**

| # | Channel / resource | Link | Focus |
|---|-------------------|------|-------|
| 1 | **The Behavior Panel** | https://www.youtube.com/@TheBehaviorPanel | Real-time analysis — critical viewing practice |
| 2 | Chase Hughes — introduction clips | Search: `Chase Hughes 6 minute x-ray introduction` | Rapid profiling framework |
| 3 | De-escalation / reading stress | Search: `Chase Hughes de-escalation behavior` | Body before words |

**What to learn (not copy for manipulation):**

- Stress shows in the body before words
- People leak truth through inconsistency
- **Profile to understand — not to dominate** (Peegel rule)

**Paid (optional, not required):** NCI University / 6MX at chasehughes.com — only if you want depth.

**One question:** *What is this person's body saying that their words are not?*
