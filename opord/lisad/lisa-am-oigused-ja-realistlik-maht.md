**Subject:** Rights inquiry — Estonian edition of *Menschenorientierte Führung*

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing on behalf of a planned Estonian republication of Professor Rudolf Steiger's *Menschenorientierte Führung*, originally translated and published in Estonia in 1997 (Fontes, Tallinn; translator Piret Liivamägi; ISBN 9985901274).

I served as editorial lead for the Estonian defence community's adoption of this work. A revised pocket-size edition is now planned for Estonian citizens, with updated framing for civil resilience and human-centred leadership (non-military retail; institutional partners may include defence education).

We request:
1. Confirmation of the rights holder for Estonian language publication
2. Terms for a **new edition** (possibly abridged, pocket format 120×152 mm)
3. Whether we may use the existing Estonian translation or require sync with the latest German edition (ISBN 978-3-7193-1503-0)
4. Recommended literary agent or estate contact following Professor Steiger's passing (January 2026)

Initial print run estimate: **2,000–5,000** copies; institutional orders possible later.

We are **not** approaching Fontes publishing directly for rights, per your guidance.

With respect,
**Heli Illipe-Sootak**  
Editorial lead, Estonian *Inimesekeskne juhtimine*  
Raudhammas OÜ / Kodaniku Raamat MTÜ
