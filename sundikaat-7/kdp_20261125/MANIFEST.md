## What changed
- New final chapter after the interim summary, before the back matter: ET "VIIMANE PEATÜKK — Punane lõng ehk juhuslikkuse anatoomia", translated into EN RU NO SV DA FI ES PL. [F]/[O] tags, 29 sources and the Tarmo frame story are kept. The TOC, EPUB nav and NCX include it.
- The Montague/Rothschild chapter is NOT included. Section 8 of the new chapter was rewritten so it does not refer to it (sources 26–29: The Scotsman, Year Zero Filmmaking, Call Me Stormy, TIME).
- Everything else is identical to 16.11. EPUB diff: only ch_23.xhtml, toc.ncx and content.opf changed, and all images are the same. The EPUB cover is now the live variant A (portrait) Kindle cover.
- Covers: the live variant A White paperback and hardcover covers were re-spined (respine.py) to the new page counts. The front and back art is unchanged.
- PL is print only: PL/ebook is reference only. RU was dropped from publishing (22.11), but its files were updated.
